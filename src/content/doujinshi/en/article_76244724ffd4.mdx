An irresistible work full of high-contact, sensual temptations from Miyamori-san, the languid downer gal! From sneaky pranks during class to intimate play at a net cafe, and all the way to intense full-on action, the illicit and sweet-as-can-be distance between just the two of them is incredible throughout. Seeing it through the eyes of an introverted protagonist is proof that the classic tropes never fail. With a generous 70-page volume, it’s a great value for your money. Highly recommended!
