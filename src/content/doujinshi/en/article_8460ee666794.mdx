With a comet collision marking the final days of Earth, two childhood friends who have completely given up on everything decide, "Since the world is ending anyway..." and embark on an outdoor sex spree across their favorite memory spots—an apocalyptic rom-com(?) bursting with decadence and ultra-high-octane eroticism!  
  
It all kicks off with Saika's unhinged proposal: "Let's have End-of-the-World Sex!!!" over a 3-day countdown to total global collapse. While the rest of humanity quakes in terror amidst violence and despair, these two visit the places closest to their hearts—the classroom, the rooftop, the bus stop, the park, and the beach—stripping away all sanity to engage in raw, instinct-driven outdoor fun. Converting the terror of mortality directly into raw libido and going crazy on each other, this incredibly thick, private end-of-the-world lucky-pervert frenzy is ridiculously hot!
